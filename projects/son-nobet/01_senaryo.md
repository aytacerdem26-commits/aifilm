# The Last Watch — Screenplay

**Runtime:** ~120 s · **Shots:** 16 · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Language:** English V.O. (the keeper's inner voice), English subtitles

---

## 1. Logline

On the last night before his lighthouse goes automatic, an old keeper sees a light out at sea flashing his own signal back at him: every beam he has sent out in forty-one years has come back to take him home.

## 2. Theme & Tone

- **Theme:** The unseen reward of quiet work. A man who spent his life giving light to others finally has it come back to him. Retirement is shown as a handing over and a homecoming, not a loss.
- **Tone:** Melancholic, hushed, patient. The magic stays inside a grounded world. The light at sea is the only impossible thing, and everything else is tactile and ordinary.
- **Visual logic:** Cold blue night against the warm, turning beam of the lamp. All through the film the warm light travels away from the keeper, out to sea. In the last image, for the first time, a warm light stays with him, in his own hand. The automatic system's light is cold white.

## 3. Characters

### NURI (lead, only character)
- **Name note:** "Nuri" comes from *nur*, "light". It is short, easy for English-speaking audiences to say, and it fits the North Aegean setting. It also quietly carries the film's theme.
- **Age:** 72
- **Appearance (fixed details):** Tall but slightly stooped, lean. Short, fully white-grey beard; a deeply lined face with wind-reddened cheeks. Pale grey eyes, tired but warm.
  - **Costume (identical in every shot):** Dark grey wool beanie; thick cream cable-knit fisherman's sweater (roll neck); knee-length navy wool pea coat with brass buttons, worn open; dark grey wool trousers; black rubber boots.
  - **Prop:** An old brass hurricane lantern with a glass chimney. It is unlit at the start and carried by its handle or hung on a wall hook.
- **Personality:** A man of few words, meticulous and devoted to his work. He doesn't show emotion openly; his grief shows in order, in cleaning, in the way he looks at things. He is used to solitude and holds no grudge against it.
- **Voice (V.O.):** Low, slightly husky, slow and calm. He leaves room to breathe between phrases. Old but not frail, and intimate, as if spoken close to the ear. A light, unplaceable Mediterranean accent is welcome but optional.

> No other characters. No crowds, no second human figure.

## 4. Locations (3)

### A: LANTERN ROOM & GALLERY (outer balcony)
A white stone tower on the tip of a rocky North Aegean headland, with a black-painted iron top. The lantern room is an octagon walled in glass. At its centre is a large honey-coloured Fresnel lens, layered glass prisms, with a warm yellow-orange light turning inside. There are brass fittings and an iron floor with peeling paint, and salt stains streak the glass walls. In one corner stands a brand-new white metal electrical cabinet that looks out of place, with a single small green indicator light and no labels or text. On the wall is an iron hook for the lantern. A glass door leads out to a narrow circular gallery with an iron railing, the black sea far below, and wind.

### B: SPIRAL STAIRCASE (inside the tower)
A narrow cast-iron spiral staircase winding down through the tower. The curved stone walls have flaking whitewash, and there is one small slit window. The only light comes from the keeper's lantern and cold blue moonlight through the window. Seen from above, the spiral narrows downward like the inside of a seashell.

### C: EXT. ROCKY HEADLAND & COASTAL PATH
Dark grey, wet, weed-covered rocks run from the foot of the tower into the sea, and waves break over them in foam. A narrow dirt path curves inland from the tower, lined with dry grass. **Night:** navy-black sea, a moon between clouds, the beam sweeping across the water. **Dawn:** pale blue-grey sky with a thin pink line on the horizon. (Wide exterior shots of the lighthouse belong to this location.)

## 5. Screenplay

---

### SCENE 1: EXT. ROCKY HEADLAND – NIGHT

**S1 (8 s)**: Opening frame. Extreme wide on a pitch-black, restless sea. The lighthouse beam sweeps slowly out of the dark toward camera and floods the frame gold for a moment. As it passes, the white tower stands in silhouette at the tip of the headland, under the moon.

> NURI (V.O.)
> Forty-one years. The same light. The same sea.

---

### SCENE 2: INT. LANTERN ROOM – NIGHT

**S2 (8 s)**: Medium-wide. Nuri stands beside the turning Fresnel lens, wiping its glass with a cloth in slow, careful strokes. Light passes over his face and coat in a steady rhythm. (Hands are a small part of the frame; focus is on his face and the lens.)

> NURI (V.O.)
> Tomorrow, a machine keeps the watch.

**S3 (7 s)**: Wide, from the far side of the room. The white cabinet sits in the foreground corner, its small green light blinking calmly. Behind it, out of focus, Nuri has stopped to look at it.

> NURI (V.O.)
> The light can turn itself now, they said.

---

### SCENE 3: EXT. LIGHTHOUSE GALLERY – NIGHT

**S4 (7 s)**: Medium, side angle. Nuri has stepped out onto the gallery and leans on the iron railing with both arms, looking at the black sea. The wind lifts the hem of his coat, and the beam passes over his head.

> NURI (V.O.)
> I never knew who my light reached.

**S5 (7 s)**: Nuri's POV, wide: a dark horizon over an empty sea. Dead centre and very far away, a small warm golden light slowly appears, hanging just above the water.

(No V.O., only wind and waves.)

**S6 (6 s)**: Close-up of Nuri's face. His eyes narrow and he leans slightly forward. The beam crosses his face and moves on; in the dark, a tiny reflection of the distant light stays in his eyes.

> NURI (V.O.)
> No ship ever sailed out there. Nothing ever did.

**S7 (8 s)**: Wide, from behind on the gallery, with Nuri's back and shoulder in the foreground and the light at sea beyond. The light flashes in a rhythm: two short, one long. The lighthouse's own beam sweeps past in the same rhythm.

> NURI (V.O.)
> That was my signal. Two short... one long.

---

### SCENE 4: INT. LANTERN ROOM / SPIRAL STAIRCASE – NIGHT

**S8 (7 s)**: Medium-wide, lantern room. Nuri comes back inside, lifts the unlit brass lantern off its wall hook and walks toward the stairwell. Behind him the green light keeps blinking.

(No V.O.)

**S9 (8 s)**: Straight-down top shot. The spiral staircase narrows below like a shell. Nuri descends slowly along the spiral, growing smaller in frame. Blue moonlight from the slit window falls across the steps.

> NURI (V.O.)
> Forty-one years looking down. Tonight, I go down.

---

### SCENE 5: EXT. ROCKY HEADLAND – NIGHT

**S10 (7 s)**: Wide, low angle. Nuri steps out of the door at the base of the tower and picks his way across the wet rocks toward the sea. The tower rises behind him and the beam turns.

(No V.O.; the waves swell louder.)

**S11 (8 s)**: Wide, from behind Nuri. He stands at the edge of the rocks with the unlit lantern hanging at his side. The warm sphere of light glides toward him just above the water, turning the crests of the waves gold.

(No V.O.)

**S12 (7 s)**: Medium, frontal. The light hangs just above the waves at the foot of the rocks. For the first time, Nuri's face is lit from below in warm gold, the only warm face in the cold blue night.

> NURI (V.O.)
> All the light I ever gave away... came back.

**S13 (8 s)**: Medium-wide, side angle. Nuri slowly raises the unlit lantern to chest height. The sphere rises from the water, drifts into the lantern's glass chimney, and the lantern comes alive with a warm, flickering flame. (No touching: the light enters the lantern on its own, with minimal hand movement.)

(No V.O.)

**S14 (6 s)**: Close-up of Nuri's face, lit from below by the warm lantern just out of frame. His eyes are wet and his lips move into a small smile.

> NURI (V.O.)
> To take me home.

---

### SCENE 6: EXT. ROCKY HEADLAND & COASTAL PATH – DAWN

**S15 (8 s)**: Wide, looking up at the tower. Pale dawn blue. The old warm orange light in the lantern room slowly fades out. For a beat it stays dark, then a sharp, cold white new light switches on and starts to turn.

(No V.O. The creak of the old clockwork stops and a thin electric hum takes its place.)

**S16 (8 s)**: Final image. Extreme wide, dawn. Frame left, the tower keeps turning its cold white beam. Frame right, Nuri walks slowly away along the coastal path with his back to us. The small warm glow of his lantern is the only warm point in the blue-grey landscape. A thin pink line lies on the horizon.

> NURI (V.O.)
> Let the sea keep its own light now.

CUT TO BLACK. (Edit: ~2 s of black plus title and credits added in the edit; no generation needed.)

**Estimated generation load (for the producer):** 16 shots × (1 keyframe + 1 video) = 32 credits, plus references (1 character + 3 locations + possibly 1 lantern prop and 1 "light at sea" look reference), about 36–38 credits in total. That fits the ~40-credit budget but leaves very little room for regenerations.

**Notes:**
- No on-screen dialogue. All speech is V.O., so there is zero lip-sync risk.
- Every V.O. line is 10 words or fewer and fits comfortably inside its shot.
- The look of the light at sea (a warm golden sphere, no flame, soft halo) must stay identical across S5, S7, S11, S12 and S13. The art director should lock it with its own reference.
